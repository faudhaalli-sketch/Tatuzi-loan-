Terms and Conditions

To qualify for TATUZI loan in Kenya 🇰🇪, you need to meet the lender's basic requirements:

  1. Be at least 18 years old.
  2. Have a registered Airtel Money account.
  3. Have an active Airtel Money line for at least 3 months.
  4. Register a TATUZI account.
  5. Build a good credit score through savings and account activity if you don't receive an initial credit limit.

Increase your chances of getting the loan:

  • Use Airtel Money regularly.
  • Deposit 20% of the loan to Airtel Money.
  • Maintain regular transactions.
  • Repay any existing loans on time.
  • Avoid having overdue loans.
